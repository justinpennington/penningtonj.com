I founded Growth7 because I was tired of paying marketing agencies for deliverables that did not move the needle. Reports full of impressions and click-through rates that never connected to revenue.

## Activity vs. Outcomes

The marketing industry has an incentive problem. Agencies get paid for doing things — running campaigns, producing content, managing ads. They do not get paid for whether those things generate pipeline or close deals. Growth7 flips that model. We tie everything back to revenue.

## What AI Adds to Growth

AI is not magic dust for marketing. But it is genuinely useful for the grind work that scales campaigns: generating ad variations, personalizing outreach sequences, scoring leads based on behavior patterns, and optimizing spend allocation in real time. The key is that the AI sits behind a strategy, not in front of it.

## The Pipeline Test

Every initiative at Growth7 gets measured against one question: did it contribute to qualified pipeline? If a campaign generates a thousand leads but none of them convert, it failed. If a piece of content generates ten leads and three become clients, it succeeded. Volume is a vanity metric unless it converts.

## Connecting Marketing to Operations

The biggest gap I see in growth marketing is the handoff between marketing and operations. A lead comes in, sales closes it, and then the delivery team starts from scratch because the CRM and the project management tool do not talk to each other. Growth7 works best when it is paired with an operational system that carries the client journey from first touch to delivery.
