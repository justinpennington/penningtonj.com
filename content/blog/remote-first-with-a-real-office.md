People ask me why Infraxio keeps a physical office in Ponte Vedra if we are a remote-first company. The answer is simple: some conversations are better in person, and having a space for them means those conversations actually happen.

## How Remote-First Works for Consulting

Our day-to-day work — code, configuration, client calls, project management — all happens remotely. The tools are good enough now that presence in a shared space adds nothing to a focused implementation session. What remote does well is deep work.

## What the Office Is For

The Ponte Vedra office is for strategy sessions, client workshops, and the kind of whiteboarding that does not translate well to a screen share. When a client is kicking off a new engagement, we bring them in. When the team needs to map out a complex integration, we use the room. It is a tool, not a mandate.

## Why Northeast Florida

I get asked about the Jacksonville-area location fairly often. St. Johns County and the broader Northeast Florida region have a growing business community, a favorable operating environment, and proximity to clients across the Southeast. For a company that works remotely with clients nationwide, the home base matters less than the quality of the team, but it is a good place to build from.

## The Rule

Remote-first means the default is remote. The office is an option, not a requirement. That distinction matters because it keeps the team productive in the mode that works best for the task at hand.
