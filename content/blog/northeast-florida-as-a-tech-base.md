When people think of tech hubs, they think of San Francisco, Austin, or Miami. Northeast Florida does not usually make the list. But for a company like Infraxio, it is exactly right.

## The Talent Question

The Jacksonville metro area, including St. Johns County and Ponte Vedra, has a growing pool of technology talent. The area benefits from the presence of financial services companies, healthcare systems, and logistics firms — all of which employ engineers, data analysts, and IT professionals. For a remote-first company that also wants local talent for certain roles, the pipeline is stronger than most people expect.

## Cost of Operations

Operating costs in Northeast Florida are significantly lower than in traditional tech hubs. Office space, salaries adjusted for cost of living, and general business expenses are all favorable. For a consulting firm where margins matter, this is not a trivial factor.

## Client Access

Ponte Vedra is a short drive from Jacksonville International Airport, which connects to every major city. For a consulting practice that serves clients across the Southeast and beyond, travel logistics are straightforward.

## Quality of Life

This one matters more than people admit. The team members who choose to live in the area do so for the quality of life: proximity to the coast, reasonable cost of living, good schools in St. Johns County, and a pace that allows for focused work without the grind of a major metro. Happy people do better work.

## Not Trying to Be Silicon Valley

Northeast Florida does not need to be the next Silicon Valley. It needs to be a good place to build and run a business with a talented team. On that measure, it delivers.
